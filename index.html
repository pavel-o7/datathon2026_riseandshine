Business Recommendations
01 · Freedom Score as Single KPI
Integrate score into CRM, campaign manager and personalization engine. Replace fragmented metrics with one unified user health indicator. Track weekly score migration across segments.

Deploy churn model in real-time scoring. Trigger retention flows at churn_prob > 0.7. Expected: prevent 30% of at-risk users = save ₸1.8B ARR. Payback period < 3 months.
03 · Cross-sell Top-20% Targeting
Target propensity top-20% with financial product campaigns. Expected 2–4× conversion uplift vs random. Segment by NBA output for product-specific messaging (FTravel, FMedia, FBank).
04 · Channel Budget Reallocation
Shift spend to FTravel (LTV/CAC 4.7×) and FBank (lowest churn 12%). Reduce Arbuz and Insurance budget. Estimated CAC reduction: 18% while maintaining user quality.
05 · App Adoption Drive
Users with 3+ apps churn 3× less. Use NBA engine to drive cross-app discovery. In-app recommendation banners + first-purchase cashback for new apps. Target: raise avg unique_apps from 1.8 → 2.5.
